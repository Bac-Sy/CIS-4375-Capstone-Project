# Space City Couture — User Guide

**Handoff Date**: November 16th, 2026
The information within this file is also located in the document provided with your software. *For further assistance*, please reach out to ModuNet within the appropriate hand-off period. 

# Available Pages
- Dashboard
- Inventory
- Orders
- Reports
- Employees
- Settings
- Manage Catalog (MANAGER ONLY)

## 1. Logging In
In order to log into your system, you will open the website provided by *ModuNet*, and a local version of your software will load within your browser. Type in your assigned username and password into the corresponding fields. A successful log-in will take you to the dashboard. To log-out, please look at the bottom next to your name, and click that button to sign out. Confirm you have logged out prior to leaving the browser page. 

## 2. Viewing the Dashboard
Once you sign-in, you are able to view sales analytics such as units sold and note trends within your inventory. 

## 3. Checking Inventory
To check your inventory, please select the 'inventory' tab within your navigation menu. Find the product you are looking for by browsing the table. The table currently shows the name of the product, the size, the color, its location, and the quantity.

## 4. Adjusting Inventory
To adjust your inventory, locate the product you wish to update. Within that card, you may fix sizes, colors, and quantity. Once you make the adjustment, please click *Save Changes* to confirm. If you are adding a new variant to an existing item, you will click *Add X variants.*

## 5. Managing Inventory
To manage products, you may create a product from the *inventory* page. You may adjust the quantity of the item by using the *Adjust stock* column, or change color/size information by using *Edit Variant*. Additionally, you can also delete existing products by going into an item card and clicking *Delete Variant*, which will delete the size and color you created. 

## 6. Adding an Employee (MANAGER ONLY)
To add an employee, you will click *Add Employee*. Within that menu, you will complete the first name, last name, their role, username, email, and password. The initial password must be provided to the employee through a secure channel. Open the *employee* page to confirm that the employee was successfully created.

## 7. Recording Sales & Restock
To record a transaction, navigate to Orders and select *Record Transaction*. You will be able to input information regarding date, type, the product information (type, size, color), the quantity, and employee information. Please use *Submit* when completing an order.

## 8. Reviewing Orders
On the *Orders* page, you are able to view orders made and open up the page for that order number. If a mistake had been created on the order during creation, you may edit the size, color, quantity, or date, or (MANAGER ONLY) delete the order entirely. Please ensure you hit *Save Changes* to save the order. 

## 9. Settings
Within the *Settings* page, you are able to look at information regarding the current account logged in, including your full name, your username, email address associated with the account, and your role. Please ask managers regarding changes to your password. Only managers are allowed to make edits to employees. **MANAGERS** are able to adjust the stock threshold as well regarding when an item will receive a low-stock flag on their product card.
